Welcome to Summer House

A whole villa, just for your group.

Summer House Villa 51 is a private holiday home in Udaipur, close to NH-8 in the Labhgarh Resort and Shreeji Valley area. The entire villa is reserved exclusively for your booking — no shared guest accommodation. Four bedrooms, four bathrooms and generous living and dining spaces mean everyone gets room to relax.

Mornings start on a balcony or terrace with greenery and hills in view. Evenings are for the dining table, the sofas and the people you came with.

Sleeps up to 10. Four king beds plus one extra double mattress.
The villa

Rooms made for being together.

Living area

A comfortable seating area with wooden sofas, artwork and a rug underfoot, where the group can gather at the end of a day out.

Kitchen and dining

A fully equipped kitchen with a refrigerator, chimney, sink, utensils and dining essentials, next to a dining area for shared meals.

Four air-conditioned bedrooms

Each of the four bedrooms has air conditioning and a king-size bed. An additional double-bed mattress provides extra sleeping space, and the villa has four bathrooms.

Balconies and terrace

Outdoor seating with views of greenery and hills, for a quiet cup of tea or a long conversation.
